# Image sources

The library combines Japanese prints, Impressionist and Post-Impressionist paintings, an Expressionist woodcut, Chinese ink landscape, and a Cassini Saturn mosaic. Source files are embedded unchanged. Optional fitting, cropping and dithering are performed locally.

The MIT license applies to application code, not a new license claim over images. No museum or NASA endorsement is implied.

- The Met images: CC0, with `isPublicDomain: true` verified through the collection API.
- Cleveland Museum of Art: CC0, with `share_license_status: CC0` verified through its open-access API.
- NASA: attributed archival image used under [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/), not labeled CC0.
